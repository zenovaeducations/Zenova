LAND USE

Meaning and Factors
Affecting Land Use

Land Use: The way in which land is used for agriculture, forests, settlements, industries and other human activities.

Natural Factors

  • Landforms – plains, plateaus and hills
  • Climate – temperature and rainfall
  • Soil type and fertility

Economic Factors

  • Size of land holdings
  • Population and demand for food
  • Market facilities
  • Occupation and income

Social Factors

  • People's attitudes and traditions
  • Social conditions
  • Cultural and religious practices

Other Factors

  • Technology and irrigation
  • Human skill and capability
  • Land ownership and policies

TYPES OF LAND USE – PART 1

Net Sown Area
and Forest Land

Net Sown Area

  • Area under crops or cultivated land.
  • It is an important type of land use.
  • India is the second largest country in the world in terms of cultivated area, after the USA.
  • Punjab, Haryana, West Bengal, Uttar Pradesh and Bihar have more than 60% of their total area under cultivation.

Forest Land

  • Area under forests.
  • Forest cover in India: 21.71% of the total geographical area.
  • In 1950–51, it was 16.2%.
  • National Forest Policy aims to maintain 33% of the land under forest cover.
