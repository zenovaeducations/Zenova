07 / ACCOUNTABILITY

Trust is built through clarity.

We aim to communicate what the initiative includes, who it supports and how programme activity is recorded.

Clear eligibility

The criteria for Trust-supported places should be explained to students and parents before enrolment.

Clear fee information

The ₹49 registration fee, included services and optional paid advanced classes must be disclosed separately and clearly.

Programme records

Maintain appropriate records of outreach, registrations, classes, materials, assessments and Trust-supported expenditure.

Student privacy

Publish identifiable photographs, marks or testimonials only with appropriate consent.

Understand the programme before registering.

The current plan includes a ₹49 registration and optional paid advanced classes. The exact services included in registration, the scope of any Trust-supported benefit, and the price and terms of optional classes should be shown clearly on the registration page and in parent information.

COMMON QUESTIONS

A few things worth knowing.

Is Zenova Educations a trust?

No. Zenova Educations is a private educational programme led by Manjunath and the Zenova team. Devaansh Educational Trust is a separate charitable trust that supports this specific initiative for selected beneficiaries in the stated region.

Where is the current initiative focused?

The current charitable initiative is focused on selected students in Vijayanagara district, Karnataka, with initial outreach plans including Kudligi, Kottur and Hadagali taluks.

Are all Zenova courses funded by the Trust?

No. The Trust's support is limited to the specific charitable initiative and selected beneficiaries, within the Trust's approved scope. Zenova's other private educational programmes remain separate.

What is the 100-Day Student Workbook?

It is a printed planning and progress workbook intended to help students record daily targets, practice, tests, revision and learning progress.
